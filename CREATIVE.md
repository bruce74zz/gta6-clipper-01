# Creative decision — gta6-clipper-01

## The moment
- **What:** A convenience-store security camera ("FRONT DOOR" CCTV overlay) catches an
  alligator casually strolling in through the front door of a store in Leonida, while the
  clerk watches, completely unbothered.
- **From where:** Rockstar Games' official *Grand Theft Auto VI Trailer 1* on YouTube
  (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QdBZY2fkU-0), at ~47.8–48.9 s into the trailer.
  Source footage was downloaded in this session and the moment was located by scanning
  frames in this session.
- **Why it holds a viewer:** It is instantly readable in a single second — a wild animal
  doing its grocery shopping, shot like real CCTV footage. It is the trailer's most
  meme-able "only in Florida" beat: no context, no setup, and the clerk's deadpan
  non-reaction sells the joke. One moment only, not a montage.

## The cut (all text on screen is in English)
- **Format:** vertical 9:16, 1080×1920, blur-fill (the full 16:9 frame stays visible —
  clerk, counter, door and gator — over a blurred, darkened copy of itself), ~10 s total,
  so it publishes as a YouTube Short.
- **Hook (frame 1):** the clip opens *on* the gator already coming through the door,
  slowed to 40% speed so viewers can register what they are seeing.
  On-screen text: "ONLY IN LEONIDA" / "a gator just walked into a store in GTA 6"
- **Replay:** the same shot once more at normal speed, captioned "yes, really."
- **Pitch (end card, ~6.5 s, dark card, text centred in the safe area, clear of
  YouTube's bottom and right-side UI):**
  - "Make clips like this with AI and earn money on make.fun" (shown first, ~3 s)
  - "make.fun/bounty/gta6-clipper-01" (joins underneath, both stay up to the end)
- **Voice:** none — on-screen text only (allowed by the kit).
- **Audio:** the trailer's own music under the moment, kept at normal speed.

## Title
"A Gator Just Walked Into a Store in GTA 6 🐊 #GTA6 #Shorts"
